01 — About

Engineer first. Operator when it matters.

Six years inside eCommerce — first as a full-stack developer building Laravel and MERN platforms, then deep into the Shopify ecosystem: themes, custom apps, App Functions, Checkout Extensibility, and headless storefronts on Remix and Hydrogen.

I’ve shipped work on stores ranging from Patagonia and Perfect Snacks to fast-growing DTC brands. Most recently I led web operations at ZulayKitchen, where I drove a 50% Q4 revenue increase year-over-year through CRO, A/B testing, and rebuilding the parts of the funnel that were leaking conversions.

I work best when I own the problem end-to-end — architecture, implementation, performance, and the conversation with whoever is going to use the thing. I write clean code, mentor junior developers, and care about shipping over shipping perfectly.

Right now I’m open to senior full-time roles and select project work. If you’re hiring or have something interesting to build, let’s talk.
