01Founder-led SaaS2026

Akteora

A multi-tenant platform that collects video, audio and written testimonials through one link and turns them into widgets, public walls and captioned reels. AI picks the best moments but never writes a word.

  • Video rendering on a 2-core server without slowing the API: health checks stayed under 150 ms during transcodes.
  • Media never touches the API. Browsers upload straight to Cloudflare R2, and widgets read static files from the CDN.
  • The AI returns timestamps only, each one checked against the transcript, with a deterministic fallback.
  • Recording page in 23.6 KB of JavaScript, under a budget that CI enforces. About 1,700 tests.

03 / How I work

From problem
to production.

The same four steps whether it's a new product, an AI feature or an integration. Each one ends with something people can see.

01 · Understand

Start with the people and the data.

Map the real workflow, the data it touches, and the one number that proves it got better.

02 · Design

Write the decision down.

A short scope and decision record: the options, the choice, the trade-off, and what's deliberately out.

03 · Build

Make the rules enforceable.

Types, lint rules, tests and CI gates hold the important constraints, so they survive the next change. Retries, rate limits and audit trails are part of the build, not a later phase.

04 · Measure

Prove it, then hand it over.

Evals for anything AI, a before-and-after metric, and a runbook the team can own without me.
