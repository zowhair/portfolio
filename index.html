03 / How I deploy

From problem
to production.

The same four steps whether it's an AI agent, an integration or a new product. Each step ends with something the customer can see.

01 · Discover

Sit with the people doing the work.

Map the real workflow, the data it touches, and the one number that proves it got better.

02 · Scope

Agree what done looks like.

A one-page scope: success metric, what's in, what's out, risks, and what I need from the team.

03 · Integrate

Build inside their stack.

Their data, their auth, their cloud. Retries, rate limits and audit trails are part of the build, not a later phase.

04 · Prove

Measure it, then hand it over.

Evals for anything AI, a before-and-after metric, and a runbook the team can own without me.
